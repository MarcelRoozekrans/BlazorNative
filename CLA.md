Thank you for contributing to the Project. To keep the Project's licensing options
open (including the ability to offer a dual-licensed or commercial edition alongside

receive unchanged, the Maintainer asks every contributor to agree to the terms below.
You keep the copyright to your Contributions; you grant the Maintainer a broad license
to use them.

There are two variants:

- **[Individual CLA](#individual-contributor-license-agreement)** — if you are
  contributing as yourself.
- **[Corporate CLA](#corporate-contributor-license-agreement)** — if your
  contributions are made in the course of work for an employer, or the copyright is
  otherwise owned by a company.

## How to sign

The intended mechanism is a **CLA-assistant check** that comments on a contributor's
first pull request and records agreement automatically (this is enabled on the
repository by the Maintainer). Until that check is active, a contributor may signal
agreement by:

- stating in the pull request that they have read and agree to this CLA (Individual),
  and/or
- for a Corporate CLA, emailing the completed Schedule A to the Maintainer at the
  address in the package metadata.

Agreement is recorded once and covers Your future Contributions to the Project.
