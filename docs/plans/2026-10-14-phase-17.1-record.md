**The no-flow scenario, corrected before the run above.** The first version registered its
continuation under `ExecutionContext.SuppressFlow()`. It failed at its own anchor: a continuation
that captured no context runs inline in the context that completes it, which is B's. So it flowed
B's scope, not none. The fact now runs its continuation in the execution context captured at the
probe's mount, when no dispatch was open. The anchor reads the renderer's own
`FlowingScopeIsSetForTests`.
