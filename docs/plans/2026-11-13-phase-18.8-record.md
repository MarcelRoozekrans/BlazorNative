## §0 Harness

The harness lives only on scratch refs and is never merged into `feat/18.8-pipe-holder`.

**Files, on the scratch refs only:**

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `scripts/scratch-530/watch-holder.ps1` | The watcher. Polls the process tree under `-RootPid`, fires on trigger A, a generator `pwsh` with no living child for 30 s, or trigger B, a descendant that outlives its generator `pwsh` by 30 s and shares a pipe with the `pwsh`'s parent. Each firing writes `stall-<n>/` and a line in `stalls.csv`. |
| `scripts/scratch-530/plant-holder.ps1` | The planted holder for the self-test. `-Mode Parent` reads a native child's output; `-Mode Holder` is that child: it starts an inheriting `ping` sleeper and exits. |
| `.github/workflows/scratch-530.yml` | Triggers on `push` to `scratch/530-**`, `windows-latest`. Job `selftest` here; Task 2 adds job `experiment`. |

**Scratch refs:**

| Ref | Content |

| `scratch/530-holder-fixed` | Task 2: the self-test ref plus the loop commit. |
