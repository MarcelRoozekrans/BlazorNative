### Notes on the reds

- **The plan's M-list maps to the ids above:** M1 to M8 and M10 to M13 by name. M9, "the window not
  releasing holds for an unrendered tag", has no form of its own under R10: every hold is released
  at the outermost close whatever became of its tag, so M9 is the release removed, which Task 5 called
  `m_release`. M5 is Task 5's `m_tag`, M6 its `m_rule`, M12 its `m_done`. Task 5's `m_notag` is a
  subset of M8 and reds the same anchor, `:1559`.
- **M11 is not an assertion-site red, by ruling R2.** A wrong name in the check makes `BlazorInterop`'s
  static constructor throw `BlazorVersionMismatchException` naming the field, and every fact that
  touches the class fails in its setup. That is the load-time check's proof. The check's own sites,
  `:1304` and `:1307`, are entered by P1 and P2 at their own lines.
- **MG is one logical mutation.** The pin's subject is that forcing Blazor's Debug log writes nothing
  new through `BnLog`. That rests on two gates in series, the logger's and `BnLog.Write`'s, so removing
  one alone is equivalent, as Mlog1 shows. L1 is a single-statement subject red of the same site, the
  logger forwarding at the wrong level, so the site does not rest on MG.
- **D1's count reds are deterministic since `f6358d40`.** The two sends run one after the other in
  one continuation. The export hands the test tap its Task before it returns, and the continuation
  completes it after the second send. A fact waits for the first notice, then `WaitForNoticeSends` waits
  for that Task, so the count is read after both sends. On `57d8c543` this held by timing alone. All
  7 sites redded in each of two runs on `c1e6e006`, two on `f6358d40`, two on `fcd7fac6` and two on `c18dfd5b`.
- **M7 and M13 race the pool, apart from `:1406`.** Without a hold, whether A is Done before B's
  batch renders depends on when A's pool continuation runs. Their arrival reds therefore move
  between runs: the Dispose fact's `:1585`, the strict twin's `:1358` and the Rc2 fact's `:1431`
  came and went across the five sweeps. None is a cited red; C0 is. M13's red at `:1406` is forced by the seam every run.
- **E1, E2, S3 and R0 are synthesized bug shapes**, as 17.11's N2 and N3 were. Each is one bug: a
  completed handler reported as faulted; a completed handler reported as pending; a pending Task that
  resumes late; a window whose batch is not rendered before its dispatch returns.
- **The fixture and planted reds, X1 to X6, and the argument RS1 clause 3 requires.** Their sites are
  anchors on what Blazor itself does:
  - `:1692`, `:1693` and `:1736`: Blazor disposed a removed child;
  - `:1737`: the thrower re-rendered;
  - `:1561`: the target was disposed;
  - `:1562`, `:1644`: the render order inside B's batch.
