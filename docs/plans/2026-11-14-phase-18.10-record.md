## 1. The cause, and the decision

Copied from the design's "Why" section.

#444 is the iOS lane's recurring red. With 18.1's instrumentation, run 40642166828 showed SpringBoard taking 11.25 s to deliver `willPresentNotification` to the app. The app answered about 50 ms after delivery. An immediate request's `add` completes only after that exchange, and `runAction` completed the host call inside `add`'s completion. So `Granted` meant "presented and answered", and a component we do not own decided when it arrived. Run 40624968569 had already held a delegate's `willPresent` for 15 s on this lane, 3 of 3, which is the technique the held pin reuses.

Android's `runNotificationAction` (`AndroidShellBridge.kt:784-789`) completes `Granted` right after `postNotification` returns, which means "posted".

| Question | Decision | By |
|---|---|---|
| What does `show`'s `Granted` mean? | Posted: the request is handed to the center. A later `add` error is logged, or routed to the runtime's `onError`, and not returned. | Owner, 2026-11-14 |
| Does `schedule` change too? | No. A scheduled request is not presented, so its `add` never waits on SpringBoard and has never flaked. Its `add` error stays data. | Owner, 2026-11-14 |
| How? | Complete right after `add` is called. A 1 s bound (approach 2) and an app delegate that answers at once (approach 3) were rejected: a timeout is a workaround, and the stall is before delivery reaches the app. | Owner, 2026-11-14 |
| The iOS baseline | 307: a ruling added a fourth pin, `testALateShowAddErrorReachesTheRuntimesOnError`, so the two routes of the late error each have a pin. | Owner, 2026-11-14 |

## 2. The fix and its commits

`BnNotifications.runAction` branches on the action after building the request. `show` calls `add`, then completes `Granted` at once; its `add` completion keeps the #444 trace and, on an error, reports `"notifications 'show' add failed (request <id>, notification <identifier>)"` through `BnRuntime.shared?.onError`, or `BnLog.error` when no runtime is booted, the routing `AppleShellBridge.deliverFaultNotice` uses. That completion captures no handler state and never calls `complete`. `schedule` is unchanged. A test seam, `BnNotifications.addForTest`, replaces only the call to the center, so the completion the test hands back is production's.
