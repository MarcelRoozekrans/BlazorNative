### Notes on the reds

- **N2, N3 and N25 are synthesized bug shapes,** of the same kind as 17.9's B6 and X1. Each is one
  bug, even where its diff touches two or three places:
  - N2 is a late notice charged to the window it surfaced in.
  - N3 is a window whose first-fault slot is taken by another dispatch's fault.
  - N25 is a dispatch deduplicated by call site.
- **N3 against #561's hint.** The hint reads "B's own synchronous fault dropped when the scope already
  holds a captured fault". On the correct code B's scope never holds A's fault, so that guard alone is
  an equivalent mutant for this fact. N3 makes the window hold A's fault, in its own late slot, and
  then drops B's fault behind it. That is the bug the site's message names: "not lost behind A's".
  A's fault still reaches A as a late notice, so only the rc is wrong.
- **N25 stops only the second statement.** It skips the second dispatch at a call site, so in each
  of the four facts the first handler runs, the anchor's first line passes, and the second line reds.
  This is the brief's rule for a two-line anchor.
- **N8 reds three sites in one run.** N10 and N11 are separate sites in separate facts, so each is
  recorded as its own row.
- **Incidental reds, not counted.**
  - N1 also reds `ACallBegunByAnInlineContinuation_DoesNotMarkTheWindow` at `:1002` and the Rc2
    fact at `:981`.
  - N2 also reds the Rc2 fact at `:984` and `ACallBegunByAnInlineContinuation_…` at `:1004`.
  - N3 also reds `ACallBegunByAnInlineContinuation_…` at `:1000`.
  - N8 also reds the four wrapper facts through `AssertNamesTheAppsHandler`, `:640`, the get-set fact
    at `:804` and the one-page bound fact at `:770`.
  - N25 also reds six other facts that dispatch one call site twice.
