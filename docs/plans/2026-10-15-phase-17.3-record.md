- `HostSession.TryMount`: a mount replaces any root as one render-thread unit, through the

  `NativeRenderer.RootGeneration`.
- Each dispatch scope records the generation it began under. `NativeNavigationManager` drops
  `NavigateToAsync` and `NavigateBackAsync` called from a replaced root's code, and logs a Warn.

**A sixth fact, from the first device run.** Android run 38704825969 on `99114b7b` failed 9 of
240. Freshly launched Activities in one test process opened on the page an earlier test left the
session on: "stay on the page" treated every new Activity as a recreation. The owner decision
covers a recreated shell, so the fix tells the two apart at the shell:
- `MainActivity` saves its route in `onSaveInstanceState` and restores it as the bridge's initial
  route. A fresh launch has no saved state and starts at its deep link or `/`.
- A replacing mount of the default component asks the shell for its route through the existing
  `CurrentRoute` query, `NativeNavigationManager.AdoptTheShellsRoute`. The same route as the
  session's means recreation: page and history kept. A different one means a fresh launch: the
  history is reset and the shell's route is mounted.
- `ASecondMount_ByAFreshShellAtTheRoot_ShowsTheRootAndResetsHistory` was red first:
  `a fresh shell at '/' was shown [settings-marker]: it must start at its own route, …`.
- **M6**, the adopt call removed, reds that fact alone: 1 of 24 in `RecreationTests` and

**A seventh fact, from the second device run.** Android run 38707338246 on `7b618c99` failed 1

**The hold changes a documented contract.** `blazornative_register_frame_callback` said "last wins".
It now says a registration while a page is mounted is a new shell, and frames are held until its
mount. Five `ShutdownQuiescenceTests` pins re-register over a live root without mounting, because
their subject is the callback epoch and the gate. They run with
`HostSession.HoldForTheNewShellsMountForTests` off.

**Declined to judge, ruled:**
- A fresh launch at the session's route counts as a recreation and keeps that back stack. Stands: by route the two cannot be told apart, and keeping the back stack is the conservative outcome.
- A late navigation from a disposed page is lost. Stands: owner decision 3.
- Two live shells under `singleTop`, and a shell route that disagrees after a failed swap, predate 17.3 and are outside it.
- A failed mount keeps the adopted route. Stands: the shell asked for that route, and a retried mount sees the routes equal.
