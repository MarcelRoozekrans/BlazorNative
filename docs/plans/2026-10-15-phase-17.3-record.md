## 3. Mutations

**The .NET fix.**
- `HostSession.TryMount`: a mount replaces any root as one render-thread unit, through the
  `InvokeAsync` and `RunAfterDispatch` shape of the navigation swap. A mount of the default
  component re-resolves it from the current route. Every shell mount bumps
  `NativeRenderer.RootGeneration`.
- Each dispatch scope records the generation it began under. `NativeNavigationManager` drops
  `NavigateToAsync` and `NavigateBackAsync` called from a replaced root's code, and logs a Warn.

The 4 facts are green.

**A fifth fact, from executing the plan.** The planning review warned that every later `start()`
would deliver the old root's removal frame to the new runtime. Those patches describe the **old**
shell's views, which are gone, and 15 JVM test files read `frames.first()` as the mount frame. The
cause is fixed, not the tests: a replacing mount disposes the old root through
`NativeRenderer.UnmountReplacedRoot`, which updates the renderer's tree but delivers no frame for
that batch. A navigation swap keeps its removal frame, because there the shell is the same one.
- `AReplacingMount_SendsTheNewShellNoRemovalFrameForTheOldRoot` was red first:
  `… frames after it: [RemoveNodePatch,CommitFramePatch | CreateNodePatch,…]`.
- **M5**, the drop disabled, reds it alone.
- No shell checks that frame ids are consecutive: `grep` over the Kotlin and Swift frame
  consumers.
- Full suite: **1328/0**, with Runtime 1154.

**A sixth fact, from the first device run.** Android run 38704825969 on `99114b7b` failed 9 of
240. Freshly launched Activities in one test process opened on the page an earlier test left the
session on: "stay on the page" treated every new Activity as a recreation. The owner decision
covers a recreated shell, so the fix tells the two apart at the shell:
- `MainActivity` saves its route in `onSaveInstanceState` and restores it as the bridge's initial
  route. A fresh launch has no saved state and starts at its deep link or `/`.
- A replacing mount of the default component asks the shell for its route through the existing
  `CurrentRoute` query, `NativeNavigationManager.AdoptTheShellsRoute`. The same route as the
  session's means recreation: page and history kept. A different one means a fresh launch: the
  history is reset and the shell's route is mounted.
- `ASecondMount_ByAFreshShellAtTheRoot_ShowsTheRootAndResetsHistory` was red first:
  `a fresh shell at '/' was shown [settings-marker]: it must start at its own route, …`.
- **M6**, the adopt call removed, reds that fact alone: 1 of 24 in `RecreationTests` and
  `BackStateNoticeTests`.
- Full suite: **1329/0**.

**A seventh fact, from the second device run.** Android run 38707338246 on `7b618c99` failed 1
of 240: `warm_tap_reroutes_the_live_session_via_navigate_host_event` launches with a
`/notifications` deep link and `EXTRA_COMPONENT=BnDemo`. BnDemo is the default page, so the
replacing mount resolved it from the shell's route and showed the notifications page. The shell
sent a route and a component that disagree. The fix makes the shell's route name the page it
mounts:
- `MainActivity`: an `EXTRA_COMPONENT` seeds its own route, and a recreated Activity's saved
  route outranks its launch Intent's deep link, and picks the page it mounts. Without that a
  deep-linked Activity that navigated away and rotated went back to its linked page and lost its
  back stack. `a_recreated_deep_linked_activity_stays_on_the_page_it_navigated_to` pins it; lane
  mutation L4 is its red.
- .NET: every replacing mount adopts the shell's route, by name too. A fresh shell's named mount
  kept the earlier shell's back stack before.
- `ASecondMount_OfANamedPageByAFreshShell_ShowsItAndResetsHistory` was red first:
  `a fresh shell's named mount inherited an earlier shell's back stack`.
- **M7**, the adopt limited to the default page again, reds that fact alone: 1 of 25.
- Full suite: **1330/0**.
