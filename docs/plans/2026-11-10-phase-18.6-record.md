### Bounds

The rule: per-test at least 3x the slowest test (6.4 s), task at least 2x the step (192 s) and
under 45 minutes. The proposed 120 s per test and 25 minutes for the task satisfy both with a wide
margin (56x and 15x), and a cold runner has room. The measurement does not contradict them, so
they stay: `120 s` per test, `25` minutes for the task.

The job budget. In run 42838278672 (success, head `64c3c6f5`, `build-test` job 11 min 18 s) the
steps before the JVM step took 397 s, about 6.6 minutes, with the JVM step itself at 64 s, and the
steps after it, the consumer smoke, the template smoke and the uploads, took about 279 s, 4.7 minutes.
If the 25 minute backstop fires, the JVM step ends about 31.6 minutes into the job and the rest
brings it to about 36.3 minutes, 8.7 minutes under the 45 minute job limit. That is the worst case
with the caches warm. A cold restore of the NuGet, Gradle and NDK caches was not measured, and it eats
into the 8.7 minutes.
