### Bounds

The rule: per-test at least 3x the slowest test (6.4 s), task at least 2x the step (192 s) and
under 45 minutes. The proposed 120 s per test and 25 minutes for the task satisfy both with a wide
margin (56x and 15x), and a cold runner has room. The measurement does not contradict them, so
they stay: `120 s` per test, `25` minutes for the task.
