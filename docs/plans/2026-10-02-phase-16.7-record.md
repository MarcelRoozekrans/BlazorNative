## 2. The first design, abandoned

The first design forced a yield after begin, on branch `feat/16.7-rc-contract@5847d1d`; its record is `docs/plans/2026-10-02-phase-16.7-record.md` on that branch. Its reviews measured three failures of any fix that moves the handler's continuation:

- `Task.Yield()` captures the render thread's context, so app code that blocks the render thread on a host call deadlocks: `BackNoticeTest` hung for 30 minutes.
- `ConfigureAwait(ConfigureAwaitOptions.ForceYielding)` finishes the bridge on the pool and races the handler's own `await`: the inline facts went red in 7 of 15 runs.
- A page whose `OnInitializedAsync` awaits an inline-answered call no longer renders synchronously, so `Mount<T>` throws.

This phase therefore classifies the fault and leaves scheduling unchanged.
