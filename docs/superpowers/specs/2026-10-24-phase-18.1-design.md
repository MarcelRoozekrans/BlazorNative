# Phase 18.1 Design — `CI flakes at their cause`

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Phase:** `18.1`
**Surface:** `Mixed`
**Stage:** phase (one phase, implementation-ready)

**Milestone:** [M18 — Acknowledged Bugs](../../planning/MILESTONE.md)

**Scope:** #551, #564, #453, #444, #407.

**Investigation:** [the 18.1 investigation](../../plans/2026-10-24-phase-18.1-investigation.md). It records the evidence, a cause for four of the five flakes, and a local reproduction of #564.

## Goal

Fix each CI flake at its cause, so that every later M18 PR gets clean CI. M18's DoD rules out one kind of fix: no fix may raise a bound to hide a slow path.

## Approach

Each fix is pinned. Where a deterministic reproduction exists, the pin forces the race, so its red does not depend on luck.

### 1. #564: the render thread blocks on a full stderr pipe

**Cause, confirmed.** `BnLog` writes to `Console.Error` synchronously (`BnLog.cs:169`). When the test host's stderr pipe fills, `NativeRenderer.HandleException`'s `BnLog.Error` blocks the render thread inside a dispatch. The investigation reproduced this locally: 3 failures in 7 runs, each with the exact CI signature.

**Fix, by owner decision on 2026-10-24.** `BnLog`'s default sink never blocks its caller.
- Lines go to a bounded in-memory queue, and one background thread drains the queue to `Console.Error`.
- When the queue is full, a line is dropped and a counter goes up. On the next successful write, one line reports how many were dropped.
- The queue drains at process exit, best effort and bounded.

**What does not change:** the log format, the levels, and the privacy seam. Android and iOS read stderr through their pumps, so their twin pins must stay green.

**Harness fix.** When `FaultNoticeTests.DispatchProbe` throws, it skips the teardown in the caller's `finally` (`:1380`), so one stuck dispatch leaks a notice into the next fact. Make the probe's teardown run on every exit path.

**Pins:**
- **The sink.** With `Console.Error` replaced by a writer that blocks on a gate, a `BnLog.Error` returns at once, a dispatch completes within its budget, and the line appears once the gate opens.
- **Drop counting.** A full queue drops lines and then reports the count.
- **Leak isolation.** One dispatch forced past the budget leaks nothing into the next fact.

### 2. #551: the test process starves its own thread pool

**Cause, likely; mechanism confirmed locally.** The positive control's frame arrives after one hop through the thread pool (`NativeShellBridge.cs:471`, `:504`). Early in a run, process waits hold pool threads:
- the reference fixtures running `RunBounded` (`ReferenceDriftTests.cs:55`);
- `GeneratorRunTests` calling `RunBounded` inside `Task.Run` (`:67`, `:150`, `:199`), each up to 15 s;
- sleep-polling test bodies.

Measured locally, the delay before the frame equalled the pool's queue delay, to the millisecond.

**Fix.** Keep blocking process waits off the pool.
- `RunBounded` runs its waits on a dedicated thread, or becomes async.
- The reference fixtures become `IAsyncLifetime`.
- `GeneratorRunTests` uses `TaskCreationOptions.LongRunning`.

**Pins:**
- `RunBounded` called on a pool thread fails, naming the rule.
- A deterministic reproduction: with every pool thread held on a gate, the positive control still receives its frame, or the test names pool starvation. Before the fix it reds every time.

**Proof on Windows.** The fix must also survive the `ci.yml` runs this phase's PR makes, on top of 20 local runs that log the pool delay.

### 3. #453: the first `LAContext()` after a cold simulator boot takes 26–41 s

**Fix, by owner decision on 2026-10-24: both halves.**
- **The shell.** The iOS shell creates one `LAContext` at boot, so an app pays the cold cost once and early, not on its first biometric call. The boot tests wait for that warm-up as a named step.
- **The lane.** `ios.yml` boots the simulator and waits for it to finish booting before `xcodebuild test`.

**Pins:**
- An ordering pin, through `contextFactoryForTest`, shows the warm-up runs at boot and before the first biometric request.
- At least 10 fresh-boot runs on the iOS lane, all green.

### 4. #407: the Activity is recreated mid-test

**Cause, confirmed for its one hit.** About two minutes after boot, the emulator's Play-services updates changed asset paths. The OS relaunched `MainActivity` between the camera test's echo check and its image poll, and the photo's `src` is page state.

**Fix:**
- **Lane.** `android-instrumented.yml` waits for the emulator's package updates to settle before `connectedAndroidTest`.
- **Test.** The camera test's poll fails naming a recreation, when one happens, instead of timing out bare.

**Pin.** A test forces `scenario.recreate()` during the poll and checks the poll names it.

### 5. #444: the notification show never completes Granted

**Cause unknown.** The show either passes within 0.2 s or fails at the full 10 s. So a completion is lost, not slow.

**Owner decision on 2026-10-24: diagnose and fix it in 18.1.**

1. **Instrument the test.** Add:
   - the captured statuses in the failure message;
   - a timestamp on the `add` call and on its completion;
   - a 5 s probe of whether the notification daemon still answers;
   - the simulator's UserNotifications log, collected on failure;
   - a unique request id in place of the shared `"7"`.
2. **Reproduce it.** Loop the suite with `-test-iterations 30` on a freshly booted simulator.
3. **Find the cause and fix it there,** with a pin.

If 30 iterations never reproduce it, stop and ask the owner. Do not guess a fix.

## Non-goals

- Raising any bound or timeout.
- Fixing a flake by retrying it.
- Changing `BnLog`'s format or its privacy rules.

## Tests

- The full .NET suite locally, plus 20 runs of the #551 and #564 classes.
- The PR's `ci.yml` run on Windows, both device lanes, and the iOS fresh-boot loop.
- Every new pin is graded against `docs/pin-standard.md`, "Grading a pin: sites and reds".

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The async sink loses lines at a crash | Drain at exit, best effort and bounded. A fatal path may flush synchronously; the record states which paths do |
| The sink change breaks a shell's stderr pump or its privacy twin pins | Run both device lanes, and check the Android and iOS log pins by name |
| #551's fix is incomplete on Windows | Confirm on Windows CI; reopen with the pool-delay logs if it recurs |
| #444's cause stays unknown | Stop and ask the owner after 30 iterations |
| The 10 iOS fresh-boot runs are expensive | Batch them as one lane run with iterations, if the lane supports it |
