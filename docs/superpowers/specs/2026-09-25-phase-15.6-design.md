# Phase 15.6 Design — `Audit and close`

**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Phase:** `15.6`
**Surface:** `Docs`
**Stage:** phase (one phase, implementation-ready)

**Milestone:** [M15 — A Standard for Pins](../../planning/MILESTONE.md)
**Precedent:** [`docs/plans/2026-09-22-milestone-14-audit.md`](../../plans/2026-09-22-milestone-14-audit.md)

## Goal

Audit Milestone 15 against its Definition of Done, on evidence measured now on `main`, and close it
if it passes.

## Approach

`audit-milestone` is an established process. This spec records only the choices specific to M15.

1. **Everything is measured at the point of writing.** Every DoD item is re-measured against
   `origin/main` when the audit is written. A number or claim copied from a phase report, a
   ROADMAP block or an earlier audit does not count. M15 was caught more than once by claims that
   travelled through a document instead of being re-measured.
2. **Range.** M15 opened at `76728c9` (#368). "No public API, wire or ABI change" is checked by
   diffing `76728c9..origin/main` over:
   - every `src/*/PublicAPI.*.txt`;
   - `src/wire-vocabulary.json`;
   - the `UnmanagedCallersOnly` exports in `src/BlazorNative.Runtime/Exports.cs`.

   Release-please version-string commits are expected. They are listed and excluded by name, never
   by pattern.
3. **Device lanes.** `android-instrumented` and `ios` are dispatched on `main`'s HEAD. Each run's
   `headSha` is compared with that HEAD, as the DoD's own wording requires. A green lane on an
   older commit does not count.
4. **Pin population.** Two sets are counted:
   - the tree-reader population, by the census's own key and method;
   - the non-tree register under Rule 6.

   Every member of either set must appear in the census scorecard or in the register with a
   verdict: *conforms*, *fixed*, or *exempt with a written reason*. A pin with no verdict counts
   as a gap.
5. **Issues.** Each of #364, #296, #357, #297, #302, #291, #298, #356 and #365 must be closed on
   GitHub **and** backed by the commit or pin that closed it. #296 additionally needs its
   red-before-fix run cited.
6. **Carried debt (owner decision, 2026-09-25): "carry forward, filed".** Each open item outside
   the DoD is filed as its own issue **before** the audit is written, so the audit can cite the
   numbers. The items:
   - the iOS/Android stderr-pump log-level twin divergence (15.5 code question 2);
   - the remaining open 15.5 code questions, one issue each unless two share a fix;
   - the "every baselined public type is tiered or NOT-API" pin, blocked on the owner tiering the
     9 types in `docs/plans/2026-07-21-phase-11.3-api-tiers.md` §8;
   - the `BnCameraAndroidTest` 15-second Coil timeout flake, with its run;
   - #396, which already exists.
7. **Verdict.**
   - **PASS** or **PASS WITH FINDINGS** leads to `complete-milestone`. **No tag:**
     `CONVENTIONS.md` records `Milestone completion tags a release: no`.
   - **FAIL** leads to `plan-milestone-gaps`, and M15 stays open.

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| A device lane flakes on the dispatch, as the camera test and the emulator image download both did in 15.5 | A false NOT MET on "all tests passing" | Diagnose from the JUnit XML or the log before rerunning. Rerun only a failure proven to be infrastructure, and record both attempts. |
| The audit repeats a stale claim from a phase record | The audit certifies something false | Item 1: re-measure everything, and put the command beside the number. |
| A DoD item is met in letter but not in substance, as M14's central claim was | Closing on a technicality | Say "met narrowly" where that is true, as M14 did, rather than ticking the item silently. |
