# Phase 15.2 Design — `Define the auth pin's coverage`

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Phase:** `15.2`
**Surface:** `Backend`
**Stage:** phase (one phase, implementation-ready)

**Milestone:** [M15 — A Standard for Pins](../../planning/MILESTONE.md)
**Standard:** [`docs/pin-standard.md`](../../pin-standard.md)
**Issue:** **#364** — four reproduced holes in `AuthSemanticsDriftTests`

## Goal

Answer **#364** by stating what the auth-semantics scan must **cover** — which trees, which
spellings, which file kinds — and closing its four reproduced holes as **consequences of that
statement** rather than as four patches.

## Why this phase is about coverage and not assertions

#364's own closing paragraph sizes it, and it is the reason this is a design phase rather than a
bug-fix:

> This is the **fifth** review across two phases to defeat this pin with a shape the previous round
> had not tried: same-line ternary → wrapped token → string literal → new caller → now source root,
> file scope, raw-string block comment, and import alias.
>
> That is not a run of bad luck. It says the pin's *coverage* is the thing to reason about, not its
> assertions.

Every one of the five defeats was a place the pin **did not look**, not a thing it looked at and
got wrong. Its assertions have never been the weak half. So the fix has to make *"it does not look
there"* a red, and that means coverage must become a declared, asserted property rather than an
emergent consequence of two hard-coded paths.

## What scoping found, and what it changed

Three facts moved this design away from its first shape. All three were measured.

**1. The roster is a twin divergence, and the phase is the milestone's own subject one level up.**
`AuthSemanticsDriftTests` scans `src/BlazorNative.Jni/src/androidMain`.
`AndroidLogDriftTests` scans `src/main/kotlin` **and** `src/androidMain/kotlin` **and** both
template mirrors — and its doc comment quotes the Gradle line as its authority. **Two pins hold two
different answers to "what is the Android shell's source tree", and nothing compares them.** #364
reads as *"the auth pin forgot a directory"*; it is really *"the roster is a truth with several
copies and no home."*

**2. `templates/` is covered — by a different pin, which the manifest does not say.** The template
ships its own `AndroidShellBridge.kt` carrying all four auth tokens, and `auth-semantics.json`
declares `templates/` *"out of scope and unpinned by this file"*. That reads as unguarded. It is
not: `TemplateDriftTests` mirrors the file **byte-identically** — verified, both copies 1716 lines,
`diff` clean. The exclusion is correct and its stated reason is misleading.

**3. F1 is latent, not live.** No authenticator token exists in the unscanned tree today
(`src/main/kotlin`, `src/jvmHost`), verified by grep. The hole is real and reproduced; nothing is
currently slipping through it. That matters for sequencing, not for whether to fix it.

## The contract — three states, and no fourth

> Every tree that can hold authenticator vocabulary is exactly one of **scanned**, **delegated**, or
> **excluded**. **"Unmentioned" is not a state.**

- **scanned** — walked by the pin, in the roster.
- **delegated** — covered by a *named other pin*, **and the delegation is itself pinned.** A
  delegation is a claim about another test's behaviour; under the repo's own rule an unpinned safety
  claim is its most expensive bug class. `templates/` delegates to `TemplateDriftTests`' mirror, so
  a guard must assert that mirror list **still contains the file** — otherwise deleting one line
  from a list silently un-covers a whole tree.
- **excluded** — with a written reason, the way `ignored` entries already work.

This is the phase's actual deliverable. Completeness becomes an assertion over **trees**, not only
over tokens, so *"the pin does not look there"* is the thing that reds.

## The roster, and who consumes it

One home, in the mechanism this repo has already proven twice — `src/wire-vocabulary.json` (14.0)
and `src/dispatch-surface.json` (14.1).

**The roster is named SETS, not one flat list**, because the three consuming pins legitimately
want different subjects: `NSLogDriftTests` deliberately includes the Apple **test bundle** as its
positive-control anchor; `AuthSemanticsDriftTests` must not. A flat list would force one of them to
be wrong. Each pin declares which sets it consumes, in the manifest, where it is reviewable.

**Only tree-scanning pins consume it.** The distinction is load-bearing and must be stated in the
manifest, because getting it wrong is how this grows into a change nobody asked for:

| Kind | Pins | Roster? |
|---|---|---|
| **Shell-tree-scanning** — asks *"what is the shell's source?"* | `AuthSemanticsDriftTests`, `AndroidLogDriftTests`, `NSLogDriftTests` | **Yes** |
| **File-addressing** — names specific files on purpose | `BnLogFormatDriftTests`, `TextCollapseParityDriftTests`, `DeepLinkSeedDriftTests`, `DispatchSurfaceDriftTests`, `GeneratedSymbolShadowTests` | **No** — repointing these would be wrong |
| **Not shell source at all** | `ConsoleErrorDriftTests` — walks every `.cs` under `src/`, which is the **.NET** tree | **No.** It was on this list in an earlier draft of this spec and that was an error: sharing a walk is not sharing a subject |

**The roster is pinned against the build, not against itself.** The Android sets must equal the
Gradle `main` source set's `kotlin.srcDirs`; the Apple set must equal the Xcode target's sources.
That is what makes the next source-set addition red instead of silent — and this repo has already
paid for that exact silence once: the AGP 9 migration left `src/androidMain/kotlin` **uncompiled**
while the instrumented suite reported 111 passing tests, and it merged and sat there.

## The four holes, as consequences

| # | Hole | Closed by |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** | Half the Android shell unscanned | The roster + its build-derived pin. Closes it for **every** consuming pin, not just auth |
| **F2** | Caller count scoped to one file | Falls out of F1 — the count runs over the roster instead of one hard-coded path |
| **F3** | `StripComments` over-strips past a raw string | A real parse fix in `CommentStrippedSource`, with its own control |
| **F4** | An import alias defeats every dotted token | Both directions: add the bare constant names **and** ban the construct |

**F3 is a shared-helper change and must be treated as one.** 15.1 moved `CommentStrippedSource` to
`tests/Shared` and it now has **ten callers across three test projects**. A `/*` inside a `"""`
raw-string body opens a block comment that runs arbitrarily far, **over-stripping live code** —
the false-green direction. Every dependent pin's suite must be re-run and its count reported
**individually**; a shared-helper change can leave the aggregate correct while moving what one pin
sees.

**F4 gets closed in both directions on purpose.** Adding `DEVICE_CREDENTIAL`, `BIOMETRIC_WEAK` and
`BIOMETRIC_STRONG` as bare names closes the *reproduced* alias. It does not close the *class* — the
next alias invents a spelling nobody listed. So also **ban the construct**: an `import … as` alias
of an authenticator namespace in shell source reds. That is 14.0's bar, in the manifest's own
words: *divergence stops being **representable** rather than merely detected.*

## Files / Modules Touched

- **Create** `src/shell-source-roots.json` — the roster: named sets, each with language, purpose,
  and consuming pins. **The three-state coverage record lives here and only here**; every tree in
  it carries `scanned` / `delegated` / `excluded` with its reason, and a delegation names the pin it
  delegates to
- **Create** a roster-vs-build drift pin — Android sets against `build.gradle.kts`' `kotlin.srcDirs`,
  Apple set against `project.yml`' target sources
- **Create** the delegation guard — `TemplateDriftTests`' mirror list still contains
  `AndroidShellBridge.kt`
- `tests/BlazorNative.Runtime.Tests/AuthSemanticsDriftTests.cs` — consume the roster; F2's count
  over it; F4's vocabulary and the alias ban
- `tests/BlazorNative.Runtime.Tests/AndroidLogDriftTests.cs`, `NSLogDriftTests.cs` — consume the
  roster. **Not `ConsoleErrorDriftTests`** — it walks the .NET tree, a different subject
- `tests/Shared/CommentStrippedSource.cs` — F3, raw-string state
- `src/auth-semantics.json` — its `templates/` sentence currently reads as *unguarded* when the
  tree is byte-mirrored. It is replaced by a pointer to the roster's record rather than by a second
  copy of it
- `docs/plans/2026-09-22-phase-15.0-census.md` — the auth rows
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `README.md` — counts, which move together, **step name included**

## Tests

- **Every new fixed point mutation-proven against its own subject**, one mutation at a time,
  reverting between. `Assert` fails fast, so a batched run proves one mutation while still going
  red — indistinguishable from success.
- **The four #364 reproductions are the acceptance criteria.** Each is a live green mutation today
  and **must red** after this phase, re-run individually: the `ShellBridge.kt` widened-auth append;
  the new `BnRecovery.kt` caller; the raw-string `/*` control; the `Authenticators as Auth` alias.
- **The roster pin must red both ways** — a set removed from the roster while the Gradle file still
  declares it, and a `srcDir` added to Gradle that the roster does not name.
- **The delegation guard must red** when `AndroidShellBridge.kt` is dropped from the mirror list.
- **Every existing suite keeps its count** unless it gained a fact; a pin whose own suite changes
  shape has been altered rather than extended, and that is a finding.

## Out of scope

- **Runtime behaviour.** This pin reads source and never observes an authentication. A behavioural
  cross-shell pin needs a JVM test and an XCTest agreeing with each other — an unpinned twin in its
  own right. The manifest already says so and that sentence stays.
- **A Kotlin parser.** The positional-call and no-spaces-around-equals gaps named in
  `auth-semantics.json` stay open and stay disclosed. Closing them needs a parser this repo does not
  have, and inventing one inside a coverage phase is how scope escapes.
- **File-addressing pins.** They name their files deliberately; the roster is not for them.
- **The population-key decision** (census §9 / #375) — ruled to **15.4** before 15.1 began.

## Open Questions

None blocking. The scope fork — auth-only versus one roster home for every scanning pin — was
settled in favour of the roster, and the `templates/` treatment was settled in favour of
**delegation over scanning**: scanning it would double every site and ignored-count in the manifest
for a tree already held byte-identical, so delegation keeps the manifest honest at the cost of one
extra guard.

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Parsing `build.gradle.kts` is regex-over-Kotlin**, the very fragility this pin family keeps being bitten by | The roster pin reds on a harmless reformat, and the pressure is to loosen it until it sees nothing | Anchor on the `kotlin.srcDirs(` call specifically, and give it a **negative control**: a `srcDir` added to Gradle must red. Failure direction is a false red, which Rule 5 treats as a footnote — but say so at the pin |
| **F3 changes a helper with ten callers in three projects** | An aggregate stays green while one pin silently sees less | Re-run every dependent suite and report counts **individually**. This is already a standing requirement from 15.1 and it caught nothing only because it was obeyed |
| **The alias ban is broader than the auth vocabulary** | A legitimate alias elsewhere in shell source reds for an unrelated reason | Scope the ban to the authenticator namespaces, not to `import … as` in general. State the scope at the pin |
| **Three pins repointed at one roster** | A roster mistake becomes four pins' mistake at once | That is the trade being bought deliberately — one home, reviewable, build-derived. The build-derived pin is what makes the single point of failure *safer* than four hand-maintained copies, not more dangerous |
| **Delegation is a new concept in this repo** | The next author reads "delegated" as "not our problem" | The guard is the answer: a delegation that is not pinned is an exclusion wearing a better word, and the manifest must say that in those terms |
