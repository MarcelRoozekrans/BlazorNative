## Goal

Audit Milestone 16 against its Definition of Done, on evidence measured now on `main`, and close it
if it passes.

## Approach

`audit-milestone` is an established process. This spec records only the choices specific to M16.

1. **Everything is measured at the point of writing.** Every DoD item is re-measured against
   `origin/main` when the audit is written, and the command that reproduces each number sits beside
   it. A number or claim copied from a phase record, a ROADMAP outcome block or STATE.md does not
   count.
2. **Range.** M16 opened at `3757d0e` (#421). "No ABI change" is checked by diffing
   `3757d0e..origin/main` over:
   - the 80-byte bridge struct, in the C header and in its C# mirror;
   - the 10 `UnmanagedCallersOnly` exports in `src/BlazorNative.Runtime/Exports.cs`;
   - `src/wire-vocabulary.json`. The new notice ops (FaultNotice, BackState, BackUnhandled) must
     be present there, and every copy of them must be generated, never hand-edited.

   Release-please version-string commits are expected. They are listed and excluded by name, never
   by pattern.
3. **Per-item evidence.**
   - **#345:** `DispatchLaneBlockingTests` is flipped, not deleted, and the comment fix at
     `Exports.cs:504-506` landed as the first commit of the fix.
   - **#346:** the JVM `..._still_deadlocks_...` test is flipped. No main-thread caller blocks on
     .NET, including back, `onNewIntent` and iOS's `navigateDispatcher`. The iOS XCTest twin of
     the lane-blocking pin exists.
   - **Renderer ownership:** `ReportIfNotTheRenderOwnerThread` fails under test instead of only
     logging.
   - **Sync mount:** `MountSyncTests` pins the behaviour, not the type name `InlineDispatcher`, and
     13.2's `Dispose → InvokeAsync` recursion is kept as a named regression test.
   - **#8:** a fault after the first await reaches `onError`, proven on the JVM and on XCTest. The
     rc contract is written once, and every export's rc table agrees with it.
   - **#9:** closed on measurement, with that measurement cited.

   Each of #8, #9, #345, #346 and #438 must be closed on GitHub **and** backed by the commit or pin
   that closed it.
4. **Pin standard.** Every pin M16 added is checked against
   [`docs/pin-standard.md`](../../pin-standard.md) Rules 2–5 and 7. A pin whose mutations are not
   recorded counts as a gap.
5. **Device lanes.** `android-instrumented` and `ios` are dispatched on `main`'s HEAD, one run at a
   time per ref. Each run's `headSha` is compared with that HEAD, and a green lane on an older
   commit does not count. A failure is diagnosed from its JUnit XML or its log before any rerun.
   Exactly one rerun is allowed, and only for a failure proven to be infrastructure or one matching
   #444's known signature. Both attempts are recorded. A #444 hit makes "All tests passing" **MET
   NARROWLY**, citing #444.
6. **The S1 residual (owner ruling, 2026-10-01: acceptance confirmed).** 2 of 10 runs in 16.4
   logged a cold `LAContext` creation, one at 25681 ms inside the boot tests' 30 s wait. The audit
   records that margin. If no issue tracks it yet, one is filed before the audit is written, so the
   audit can cite its number.
7. **Carried debt (owner ruling, 2026-10-01: judge, then carry).** #424, #425, #426, #427, #435,
   #440 and #444 are already filed and stay open. Two of them are tested explicitly against the
   DoD items they touch:
   - #425 (`retire()` does not quiesce .NET) against "one thread owns the renderer";
   - #440 (Android host-call work runs on the render thread) against "no main-thread caller blocks
     on .NET" and the host-call contract.

   Where either contradicts an item's wording, that item is **MET NARROWLY** with the issue cited,
   never ticked silently. All seven are listed as carried debt with their numbers.
8. **Verdict.**
   - **PASS** or **PASS WITH FINDINGS** leads to `complete-milestone`. **No tag:**
     `CONVENTIONS.md` records `Milestone completion tags a release: no`.
   - **FAIL** leads to `plan-milestone-gaps`, and M16 stays open.

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| #444 or another device flake fires on the dispatch | A false NOT MET on "All tests passing" | Item 5: diagnose first, one rerun only for infrastructure or #444's signature, record both attempts |
| The JVM suite runs against a stale native DLL (#424) | A false green | Check the publish exit code and the DLL timestamp before trusting the count |
| The audit repeats a stale claim from a phase record | The audit certifies something false | Item 1: re-measure everything, and put the command beside the number |
| A DoD item is met in letter but not in substance | Closing on a technicality | Say MET NARROWLY with the reason, as M14 and M15 did. Item 7 applies this to #425 and #440 |
