## Approach

### 1. Write the rule into `docs/pin-standard.md`

Add one section, placed near Rule 7, that states the following.

**The site unit.** Item 11 grades two kinds of site:
- every labelled anchor, all of its lines included when its label covers several, as "both" does;
- every assertion that carries the claim in the test's **name**.

Summary sub-clauses and sibling statements are not graded. A phase may still record reds for them.

**The red standard.** This is RS1, from the 17.7 record §4, ratified by the owner on 2026-10-21. A red is one of three things:
1. a mutation of subject code;
2. a planted input, or a weakened detector, for drift, scan and stub pins;
3. a fixture or seam mutation, but only with a written argument that no subject-side red exists.

A red must **fail the site's own assertion with its own message**. A crash, an unrelated exception, or an earlier line failing first does not count for the site.

**Staleness.** A red is stale when its test file or subject file has changed since **the red's own commit**, not since the phase's squash. The check is `git diff --stat <red-commit> HEAD -- <test> <subject>`. A stale red is re-run before it is cited. This is the lesson of M5.

Existing cells cite this section instead of repeating it.
