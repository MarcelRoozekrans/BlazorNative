## Goal

Re-audit all eleven M17 DoD items on evidence measured now on `origin/main`, then close M17 if every item is MET or MET NARROWLY with its reason.

## Approach

17.6's method holds. Each point below is either a change from 17.6 or a point that 17.6 decided and this phase must not lose.

1. **Measure at the point of writing.** Every number is re-measured on `origin/main`, with its command beside it.
   - A phase record, the 17.6 audit, and the 17.7 record are cited only as the place where a mutation is recorded, never as evidence that something is true now.
   - The 17.6 audit's verdicts are a list of what to re-check. They are not a baseline.
2. **The range is unchanged.** It runs `23ffc4ab..origin/main`.
   - Item 10's ABI diff covers the 80-byte struct, the 10 exports and `src/wire-vocabulary.json`.
   - 17.7 touched no ABI file, and the diff has to show that.
3. **Re-judge the five narrowings, each against its DoD sentence:**

   | Item | Narrowed by | What to re-check |
   |---|---|---|
   | 4 | the cancellation fact's rc assertion | still unenterable; MILESTONE's summary now says so (17.7 §6) |
   | 5 | #537 | the hop and its pin, both halves, read on `origin/main` |
   | 6 | `retire()` unproven on a lane | the 17.7 rotation pin and its reds |
   | 6 | #511 | a retire only of a booted runtime — still open |
   | 8 | #522 | still open |
   | 9 | #530 | still open |

   An item whose narrowing is gone becomes MET. One whose narrowing stands stays MET NARROWLY, with the reason restated.
4. **Item 11, under RS1.**
   - RS1 is the red standard written in the 17.7 record, §4 "Rulings", which the owner ratified on 2026-10-21.
   - **Coverage.** Every pin M17 added — the 85 the 17.6 audit counted, plus the pins 17.7 added — has a red under RS1 at each of its anchors, and a register cell in `docs/pin-standard.md` that claims no more than its rows.
   - **Independence.** The re-auditor re-derives coverage from 17.7 §7 and the register, not from either alone.
   - **Re-reds on HEAD.** A pin's red must be re-run on HEAD if its test file or subject file changed after that red was taken. The rule is the same as 17.6's Approach 5.
   - **Named limits stay named limits.** Each is graded as found. They are the timing-shaped reds at `GeneratorRunTests :213` (13 of 15) and `:83` (12 of 15), and the 6-of-6-sampled red at `RecreationAndroidTest :116`.
   - **Fixture and seam reds.** The reds outside the subject listed in 17.7 §7 count under RS1's third clause. The re-auditor checks that each one carries its written argument.
   - **Grading:**
     - A pin or anchor with no red under RS1 is **NOT MET**.
     - A named limit is **MET NARROWLY**.
     - A register cell that claims more than its rows is **NOT MET** until it is corrected.
5. **Item 2, all tests passing.**
   - **Local .NET:** five runs of the full suite with `--blame-hang`.
   - **JVM:** CI `build-test` on this phase's PR, which is docs-only against HEAD.
   - **Device lanes:** both, on `main`'s HEAD. The 17.6 rule applies: compare `headSha`, attempt 1 only, diagnose before rerunning, and allow one rerun, for proven infrastructure only.
   - **A hit on #551,** the Windows ci flake in `ShutdownQuiescenceTests`, is diagnosed against its signature. It is recorded with both attempts, and it makes item 2 **MET NARROWLY** citing #551.
6. **Debt.** Each carried issue is judged against the item it touches.
   - **Open from 17.6:** #500, #506, #511, #522, #530, and #491 adjacent.
   - **New from 17.7:** #543, #544 and #551.
   - None blocks the close unless it contradicts an item's wording. Each is listed with the M18 phase the owner planned for it on 2026-10-21.
7. **Verdict.**
   - **PASS or PASS WITH FINDINGS:** run `complete-milestone` as its own PR. **No tag:** `CONVENTIONS.md` records `Milestone completion tags a release: no`. M18 planning follows.
   - **FAIL:** run `plan-milestone-gaps` again.
