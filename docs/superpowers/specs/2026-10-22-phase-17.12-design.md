## Goal

Re-audit all eleven M17 DoD items on HEAD. Item 11 is graded against the **written** rule in `docs/pin-standard.md`, in the section "Grading a pin: sites and reds". If M17 passes, close it.

## Approach

### 1. Items 1 and 3–10

These are re-measured as 17.10 did.

- **Code changes.** `git diff --stat d31e9989 origin/main -- ':!docs' ':!*.md'` shows what changed outside the docs since 17.10's local run. Show it, then re-read each code quote on HEAD.
- **Item 5.** It stays MET: #537 is fixed.
- **Narrowings.** Items 4, 6 (#511), 8 (#522) and 9 (#530) are re-checked against their issues' states.

### 2. Item 2

- **Evidence.** The PR's own lanes, plus one local .NET run.
- **Flakes.** The #551 and #564 rules apply. A hit is diagnosed against its signature and its job is re-run once, which leaves the item MET NARROWLY citing that issue.

### 3. Item 11

Item 11 is graded against the written section and nothing broader.

1. **Population.** Every test added by the M17 squashes, re-derived from git, including #567. Expected: 87 pins.
2. **Sites.** Use the section's unit: every labelled anchor, all of its lines, plus every assertion carrying the claim in the test's name.
3. **Coverage.** Every site needs a red that:
   - follows the section's RS1;
   - fails its own assertion with its own message, quoted somewhere in a record;
   - is fresh, meaning `git diff --stat <red-commit> HEAD -- <test> <subject>` is empty.
4. **Cells.** No register cell may claim more than its rows.
5. **Independence.** The re-auditor derives the sites from git and the test files, then compares them against the 17.11 record §4.
6. **Limits.** The named limits are graded as the section and the 17.10 re-audit §11f list them: `GeneratorRunTests :213` and `:83`, and `RecreationAndroidTest :116`. Each is MET NARROWLY.

**What is not a finding.** Anything only a broader unit would count. That is #562, planned for M18.
